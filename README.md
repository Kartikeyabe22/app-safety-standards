Child Safety Standards Policy

Overview
Our application is committed to protecting children and maintaining a safe environment for all users. We uphold a zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and any form of exploitation.

Prevention and Moderation Measures
Our app is designed for users aged 13 and above.

User-generated content is monitored and subject to moderation to ensure it aligns with community standards and legal requirements.

Inappropriate content including nudity, abuse, bullying, harassment, or exploitation is prohibited and subject to removal.

Reporting Mechanism
Users can report any content or behavior that violates our guidelines directly within the app.

All reports are reviewed by our moderation team in a timely manner.

Verified violations are subject to appropriate actions, including suspension or permanent removal of the account.

Legal Compliance
We comply with regional and national child safety laws.

Any suspected CSAM is immediately reported to the relevant authorities, including law enforcement and national reporting centers.
